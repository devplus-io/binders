Video promo Prompt example

Prompt: Create a 30-second product promo sizzle reel for [PRODUCT NAME], a [one-line description]. Audience: [who it's for]. Tone: premium, kinetic, confident.

VISUAL STYLE
- Photoreal CG product shots mixed with abstract motion graphics
- Lighting: dark studio, hard rim lights, [BRAND COLOR] accent glow, light volumetric haze
- Materials: accurate PBR (brushed metal, glass refraction, soft-touch matte)
- Grade: high contrast, deep blacks, [BRAND COLOR] highlights, subtle film grain
- Camera: macro-lens feel, shallow depth of field, smooth motivated moves only

STRUCTURE (cut to a ~120 BPM track, major hits on downbeats)
0–3s Cold open: extreme macro of a product detail emerging from darkness as a light sweep reveals texture
3–8s Build: rapid beat-synced cuts of fragments (edges, logo emboss, button press)
8–12s Hero reveal: product assembles from an exploded view, snaps together on a bass hit, slow 180° orbit
12–20s Three feature beats, ~2.5s each, each with a visual metaphor plus kinetic type (3 words max):
• [FEATURE 1] → [visual, e.g. "light trails racing through circuitry"]
• [FEATURE 2] → [visual]
• [FEATURE 3] → [visual]
20–26s In context: product in real-world use in [SETTING], match cut from CG to live action
26–30s End card: product centered, logo resolves from particles, tagline "[TAGLINE]", CTA "[URL / Available now]"

TRANSITIONS
Match cuts, light-leak wipes, whip pans with motion blur, speed ramps (slow-mo into 2x). No preset transitions.

TYPOGRAPHY
[FONT or "bold geometric sans"], all caps, wide tracking, mask-reveal animation, max 1.5s on screen per card.

SOUND DESIGN CUES
Whooshes on camera moves, sub-bass hit on hero reveal, crisp UI clicks on feature beats, riser into end card.

DELIVERABLES
16:9 master (4K, 24fps); 9:16 and 1:1 social reframes with the product kept center-safe; 15s and 6s cutdowns.

AVOID
Products floating without contact shadows, warped or misspelled logos, cluttered frames, generic stock-footage look.
