Keep an envelope-encrypted history of state revisions (`terraform_state_versions`, capped at 20 per deployment), tighten lock verification on state writes, expose scrubbed resource/output diffs in `devplus-backend`, and let project/tenant admins roll back to a prior state serial (browser session + 15-minute step-up auth + fail-closed `audit_events` row, incrementing serial to `max + 1` and syncing `Deployment.resources` and `Deployment.outputs`). Full specification: [[State Versioning, Diffs, and Rollback]].
